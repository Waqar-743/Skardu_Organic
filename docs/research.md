# Research — 11 October 2026

## Scope and evidence
Research preceded implementation. Existing repository inventory was mock data, not confirmed business inventory. Prices and five product IDs were retained as clearly labelled samples. No farmer identities, customer reviews, certification or medical claims have been invented.

### Regional context
- [Gilgit-Baltistan Tourism Department: Skardu](https://e-tourism.visitgilgitbaltistan.gov.pk/districts/skardu): official geographic and tourism context. Use Baltistan/Skardu language rather than interchangeably labelling every scene Himalayan or Hunza. The generated landscapes are not evidence of a specific location.
- [AKTC: Shigar Fort Residence](https://the.akdn/en/publication/aga-khan-trust-culture-shigar-fort-residence-baltistan): documents a Balti cultural museum and refined woodcarving. Design inference: warm timber/stone tones, precise framing and restrained linear detail are appropriate references. No invented traditional motif is called authentic.
- [AKDN: Timber, Rock and Stone](https://the.akdn/en/resources-media/multimedia/video/timber-rock-and-stone): describes conservation around Shigar and Khaplu forts. The site discusses regional heritage, without claiming the store funds or participates in conservation.
- [AKRSP](https://akrsp.org.pk/) and [Agriculture and Food Security](https://akrsp.org.pk/agriculture-and-food-security/): community development and agriculture work in Gilgit-Baltistan and Chitral. This supports contextual research, not a partnership claim.
- [FAO, Women in Agriculture in Pakistan, p. 77](https://openknowledge.fao.org/3/a-i4330e.pdf): discusses sulphur drying of apricots and shelling apricot kernels/walnuts for consumption, oil extraction and sale. Do not assume all dried apricots are sulphur-free or all oils are cold-pressed.
- [Pakistan Planning Commission, Apricot Cluster Report](https://pc.gov.pk/uploads/report/Apricot_Cluster_Report.pdf): apricot production includes Gilgit-Baltistan; perishability makes preservation relevant. Harvest and drying form the narrative rather than unverified health claims.
- [Visit Skardu culture](https://visitskardu.pk/culture): reviewed as a secondary reference. Its production share, antiquity and language claims were not independently validated and were not reproduced.

Language, clothing, weaving and embroidery are not used as decorative shorthand. No unverified Balti translations, model portraits, ethnic costume or invented artisan stories appear. Deeper field research and local review are needed before introducing such content.

## Five commerce references
Reviewed publicly accessible homepage content/navigation via web retrieval. This is a content and information-architecture study; competitors' live motion, checkout, colours, font rendering and mobile behaviour were not browser-tested and are not claimed as verified.

| Reference | Observed presentation | Application to this store |
|---|---|---|
| [Diaspora Co.](https://www.diasporaco.com/) | Product categories, cooking context, named origins and sourcing stories | Place and ingredient context near product discovery; make provenance claims only with evidence |
| [Burlap & Barrel](https://www.burlapandbarrel.com/) | Single-origin positioning and a broad spice catalog | Clear categories and direct access to products alongside origin storytelling |
| [Graza](https://www.graza.co/) | Focused olive-oil product offering and approachable product-first copy | One hero product in a dedicated motion sequence; direct shopping controls |
| [Brightland](https://brightland.co/) | Oils and gift sets presented as distinct shopping paths | A considered product range, simple categories, clear price/size information |
| [Aesop](https://www.aesop.com/) | Extensive category navigation, formulations and editorial/product content | Restraint in copy and clear hierarchy between story and product information |

Design decisions are original interpretations, not copied layouts. Warm ivory, forest green, amber and serif typography were chosen for this project's orchard narrative. No competitor conversion or performance metrics are assumed.

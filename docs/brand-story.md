# Skardu Organic — creative direction

## Positioning
A considered collection inspired by the orchards and high valleys of Baltistan. The brand name is supplied by the repository; “Organic” is not presented as a verified certification. The voice is calm, specific and grounded in ordinary rituals rather than miraculous benefits.

Core line: **A little closer to the source.**

## Homepage narrative
1. **The mountains:** cinematic landscape illustration, immediate collection link, visible location and image disclosure.
2. **Nature’s pantry:** four sample products, direct bag controls, category tabs. Visitors can shop without completing the story.
3. **The heart of the apricot:** pinned reversible fruit/kernel/oil narrative. A transparent concept bottle scales, moves and rotates as the background changes. Scene selection changes presentation only.
4. **The orchard:** portrait illustration beside verified regional harvesting/preservation context. No fictional growers.
5. **People and place:** Balti material heritage; no fabricated business participation or artisan portrait.
6. **From discovery to home:** explains selection, device-local cart and draft review honestly.
7. **The collection:** final landscape shopping invitation, followed by practical navigation and launch status.

## Design system
- Background `#f7f5ed`; ink/pine `#263e31`; muted text `#666c60`; warm product surfaces and amber accent.
- Georgia editorial display typography paired with DM Sans for readable interface text. System fallbacks remain legible if fonts fail.
- Desktop gutter 4.5vw, constrained to 22–76px. Main content max-width 1600px. Editorial sections use asymmetrical imagery/copy; merchandise uses practical responsive grids.
- Square-edged product surfaces, quiet hairlines, restrained circle controls, spacious sections.
- Desktop headline scale 66–118px; mobile approximately 57–90px. Body content bounded to readable measure.
- GSAP owns complex hero/pinned motion. CSS owns hover, drawer and filtering transitions. ScrollTrigger is removed at mobile breakpoints and when reduced motion is requested.
- Native links and hash routes preserve browser back/forward and direct product URLs. Modal cart uses native dialog keyboard containment and Escape dismissal.

## Content boundaries
No countdown pressure, fabricated reviews, fake organic badges, medical claims or invented sales figures. Sample prices and images are disclosed near shopping interfaces. Checkout saves a device-local draft only, in accordance with the owner's instruction to leave payments out.

## Final motion revision

The owner requested restoration of the official brand and improvement of the initial animation. The long pinned scene and hero parallax were replaced by natural scrolling, a CSS sticky product stage and three readable chapters. Desktop bottle rotation is restrained (approximately -5 to +3 degrees), with subtle scale, position and background changes driven by scroll progress. No scroll interception, forced jumps or pin spacers. Mobile shows normal stacked content, and reduced-motion preference removes the animation. The official gold/green logo replaces the provisional mark throughout.
